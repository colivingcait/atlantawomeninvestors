What we're about

A room full of women who get it.

Real estate has built more wealth than almost any other asset class — and we believe women deserve a bigger seat at that table. Our meetups are a welcoming, no-judgment space to learn the ropes, ask the “dumb” questions, and meet partners who lift you up.

Learn the strategies

Buy-and-hold, BRRRR, short-term rentals, house hacking, creative financing — we break down how it actually works with local experts.

Build your network

Meet agents, lenders, contractors, and fellow investors. The right connection can turn into your next deal or partnership.

Take real action

Leave each meetup with a clear next step. We celebrate first deals, refis, and wins of every size.

Find your people

Investing can feel isolating. Here you'll find accountability, mentorship, and friends who are on the same journey.

Our story · Your hosts

We started out as strangers at a meetup.

Back in 2024, the two of us met at a women's real estate investing meetup — and hit it off immediately. We became friends, then decided to do something bolder: build a portfolio together.

Over the next couple of years we grew to 50 rooms plus long-term rentals, and started co-hosting women's investing events — including these monthly meetups. We went from two strangers at a table to business partners with our lives and goals completely intertwined.

That's the whole point of this room. Your future partner could be sitting at this table, too.

What to expect

Come as you are — here's how the night goes and what to know before you walk in.

Still have a question?

Leave your name and number and we'll text you back.

Do I need to know anything about investing?
Not at all. We get complete beginners and seasoned investors in the same room — come curious and ask anything.
Is it really free?
Yes. RSVP on Eventbrite so we can plan the space; the meetup itself is free to attend.
What time should I arrive?
Doors open at 6:30 for drinks and food; the speaker starts around 7. Come early to settle in and meet people.
Can I come alone?
Absolutely — most people do their first time. You'll be welcomed, and you won't be alone for long.
Is parking easy?
Yes, there's parking at New Realm Brewing. We'll send details with your RSVP confirmation.
