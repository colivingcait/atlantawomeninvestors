What we're about

A room full of women who get it.

Real estate has built more wealth than almost any other asset class — and we believe women deserve a bigger seat at that table. Our meetups are a welcoming, no-judgment space to learn the ropes, ask the “dumb” questions, and meet partners who lift you up.

📚

Learn the strategies

Buy-and-hold, BRRRR, short-term rentals, house hacking, creative financing — we break down how it actually works with local experts.

🤝

Build your network

Meet agents, lenders, contractors, and fellow investors. The right connection can turn into your next deal or partnership.

💸

Take real action

Leave each meetup with a clear next step. We celebrate first deals, refis, and wins of every size.

💛

Find your people

Investing can feel isolating. Here you'll find accountability, mentorship, and friends who are on the same journey.

Our story

It started with a simple question.

“Why aren't there more women in the room?” We kept showing up to investor meetups and noticing how few of us there were. So we decided to build the room we wished existed — one designed by women, for women.

What began as a handful of friends swapping spreadsheets over coffee has grown into a thriving monthly gathering of investors at every stage. From first-time buyers to seasoned landlords, we come together to learn, share wins and lessons, and grow our portfolios with people who genuinely want to see us succeed.

Whether you own ten doors or you're just curious where to start, there's a seat here for you.
